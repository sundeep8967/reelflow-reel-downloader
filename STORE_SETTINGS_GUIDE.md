# Google Play Console: Store Settings Guide

Link: https://play.google.com/console/u/0/developers/6557036077984476803/app/4975558810390900787/store-settings

Follow these exact options on the **Store settings** screen:

---

## 1. App Category
- **App or game**: Select **App**
- **Category**: Select **Video Players & Editors** (or **Tools**)
  *(Recommended: **Video Players & Editors** for highest relevant visibility)*

---

## 2. Tags (Click "Manage tags" or "Edit tags")
Select the following tags:
- **Video players & editors**
- **Video downloader** (if available)
- **Tools**
- **Productivity**
- Click **Apply**.

---

## 4. External Marketing
- **Advertise my app outside Google Play**:
  - Keep checked: **Turn on external marketing** (Checked by default so Google can feature and index your app in external search and promo campaigns).

---

## 5. Save
Click the blue **Save** button in the bottom-right corner!
